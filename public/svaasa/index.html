Why SVAASA

Bridging the gap between innovation and implementation.

Sustainability challenges are complex. Developing a technology is only the beginning.

For an innovation to create meaningful impact, it must be technically validated, tested in real-world conditions, developed into a viable venture and connected to markets for scale. SVAASA addresses this journey through a continuous pathway — moving promising sustainability innovations beyond the laboratory, through prototyping, industrial validation, venture building and commercial deployment.

Problem Discovery→Ideation→Concept Validation→Prototype→Pilot→Scale & Deployment

From Programmes to SVAASA

From identifying a problem to creating a scalable sustainable venture.

  1. TRL 1–2

    Problem Discovery & Early Research

    Students and citizens identify water and sustainability challenges and develop early-stage innovations.

    Stockholm Junior Water Prize — India
  2. TRL 2–3

    Ideation & Concept Validation

    Undergraduate students develop climate and sustainability ideas and progress them towards proof-of-concept.

    Ideas to Impact — i2I
  3. TRL 4–6

    Prototype & Industrial Pilot

    Early-stage startups and postgraduate/research teams develop functional prototypes and undertake pilot testing.

    Carbon Zero Challenge — CZC
  4. TRL 7+

    SVAASA

    Scale & Deployment. Revenue-stage startups progress towards commercial-scale market deployment and corporate off-take.
