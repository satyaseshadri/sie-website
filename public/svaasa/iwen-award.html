About the initiative

From compliance to measurable water and energy performance.

Water scarcity, rising energy costs, and increasing expectations around sustainability reporting are reshaping India’s textile sector. Presented by IIT Madras (Knowledge Partner) and Grundfos, the National I-WEN MSME Award 2026 provides a structured platform for textile dyeing MSMEs to assess, benchmark, and strengthen their water and energy performance — moving beyond compliance toward measurable resource productivity and export readiness.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Sector

Indian textile and dyeing MSMEs.

Enterprise size

Medium-sized MSMEs with annual turnover between ₹300 crore and ₹700 crore.

Why participate

What a shortlisted unit receives

Complimentary water and energy assessment

Detailed, cost-free on-site water and energy assessment by IIT Madras experts for the top 10 Stage 1 shortlisted finalists.

Performance benchmarking

Evaluate and benchmark the unit’s water, energy, and waste metrics against industry peers using a standardised IIT Madras assessment framework.

Expert support

Technical workshops and guidance from IIT Madras on water efficiency, energy management, carbon and water footprinting, and digital monitoring.

ESG, BRSR and export readiness

Build verified sustainability credentials, improve supply-chain confidence, and align with domestic and international buyer expectations.

National recognition

Official IIT Madras certification, national recognition, and publication in a sector-wide Best Practices Compendium.

How it works

From the form to the ceremony
