About

What the centre does

India's startup boom has been driven largely by policy and practice. CREST exists to add academic evidence — so investment decisions, incubation programmes and public schemes rest on data, not anecdote.

The centre studies innovation, entrepreneurship and risk capital. It builds unique datasets on ventures and investors, publishes scholarly work, and works with ministries, incubators and banks on applied studies.

SIE and CREST sit in the same campus ecosystem. Faculty who mentor SIE founders also advise CREST research, and SIE's PhD programme draws on the same questions: how ventures form, raise and scale in India.
