FAQ

Before you apply

Match it to your venture's current revenue and fundraising status: no revenue and no funding raised sits in Category 01, funding raised but still pre-revenue is Category 02, and revenue plus funding raised is Category 03.

For Category 01, teams not already part of the Nirmaan Pre-Incubation Program will need to get pre-incubated. For Categories 02 and 03, teams not already incubated will need to enroll at the IIT Madras Incubation Cell — both are conditions of the grant, not just preferences.

This cycle is focused on clean tech, agri tech, and fintech ventures — select whichever applies, or more than one if your venture spans domains.

Note your current incubation status in the application — reach out to us directly if your situation doesn't fit the options in the form.
